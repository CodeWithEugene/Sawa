## 5. All Tracks And Challenges (Full Detail)

### 5.1 Track 1: Harlem Fine Art and AI for Peace (Creators)

For artists and musicians. Challenges: AI as creative partner; Legacy in motion; The borderless studio (Harlem and Nairobi, one piece in real time); Peace, love, unity; Co-own your work (phigital piece, on-chain provenance, royalty terms agreed in advance). Deliverable: finished piece, 3-minute process video, artist statement with AI disclosure. (Pack p.2; deck slides 6 to 8.)

### 5.2 Track 2: MeTTa and Omega (Builders), our track

"One challenge per team. Levels run from MeTTa Foundation (Level 1) to Advanced, and every challenge has an art brief." (Pack p.3.)

## 16. The Technology: MeTTa, Hyperon, Omega, PeTTa

- **MeTTa**: a language for AI that reasons; programs hold knowledge as atoms, apply logic by pattern matching, and can show how they reached a conclusion (pack p.2).
- **Hyperon**: the engine that runs MeTTa (Python package `hyperon`).
- **Omega** (`singnet/Omega`): an agent framework on MeTTa that gives agents memory, auditable reasoning and the ability to update their own rules; ships a NAL library (`lib_nal.metta`) for truth-valued deduction and revision (pack p.2; research.md).
- **PeTTa**: a fast MeTTa implementation on SWI-Prolog that Omega's own image uses.
- Full verified technical notes: [research.md](research.md) section 3.

## 17. What Wins First Place Here

1. **Qualify completely.** Repo, docs (what, how, next, AI disclosure), video of 3:00 or less showing it working and naming what's next, registered profile, on time.
2. **Be the literal answer to one challenge.** "Two agents disagree about a fact and reason to a shared answer": our demo opens with two agents disagreeing and ends with one shared answer that both humans sign.
3. **Use the art brief, not just the challenge.** The organisers added art briefs this round; most builder teams will ignore them. We build for AI for Peace, Harlem and Nairobi, and Incantio's split sheet.
4. **Make Omega essential.** NAL revision merges two memories; defeaters discount evidence; memory of earlier co-writes changes the answer; every number has a clause trail anyone can contest.
5. **Make the outcome concrete.** A percentage on a split sheet that changes for a reason you can see is clearer on video than a paragraph of text.
6. **Ship it live and polished.** Deployed URL, a designed interface, a tested scenario.
7. **Video like last time:** narrated, captioned, fast, one hero story, the "why Omega" moment, and "what's next".

## 20. Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Split sheet** | A short signed document listing every writer of a song and each one's percentage share of the composition (and, separately, of the master) |
| **Composition** | The song itself: melody, lyrics, harmony. Earns publishing royalties |
| **Master** | A specific recording of the song. Earns master royalties |
| **Sync** | Licensing music for use with picture (film, ads, games) |
| **NAL** | Non-Axiomatic Logic; reasoning with truth values (frequency, confidence) under limited knowledge |
| **Truth value `(stv f c)`** | Frequency f (how often the statement held in the evidence) and confidence c (how much evidence there is) |
| **Revision** | NAL rule that merges two independent pieces of evidence about the same statement |
| **Defeater** | A rule that discounts a piece of evidence because of another fact (for example, a claim made after a dispute began) |
| **Phigital** | Physical and digital linked: the original stays physical, a digital twin carries proof and permissions |
| **EAT** | East Africa Time, UTC+3 (Nairobi) |
