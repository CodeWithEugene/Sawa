## Description

**What it does.** When two artists co-write a song, they remember it differently, and until they agree on the split nobody gets paid: collecting societies hold royalties on conflicting registrations and licensing platforms cannot pay out. Sawa gives each co-writer a SingularityNET Omega agent that remembers only their side of the session (voice memos, project files, lyric edits, chats). In the demo, a Harlem producer's version gives him 77.5% and a Nairobi songwriter's gives her 40%. The agents share file fingerprints and messages, never audio, and each one works out the split on its own. Both reach the same answer, Wanjiru 39.7% and Malik 60.3%, with the same agreement ID. Every share explains itself in English or Kiswahili, either writer can challenge any evidence and watch both agents recalculate, AI-generated parts (an AI drum groove) are disclosed and owned by no one, a voice model needs the singer's recorded consent, and nothing counts until both writers sign.

**How it works.** Two Omega agents, each with its own Hyperon atom space and an append-only memory replayed on every request. Each piece of evidence gets its truth value from one NAL deduction through Omega's unmodified lib_nal.metta; three defeaters (received before created, memory written after the dispute, edited after the AI made it) re-target or weaken evidence; NAL revision merges what remains; Omega's action threshold decides what is settled; the writers' own split policy (beat 50, topline 50, from industry practice) turns settled facts into shares; the proof is hashed, and the two agents must produce the same hash. TypeSafe Jev only reads chat messages (Sheng, Swahili, English) into typed credit statements that the author confirms; no language model decides or explains a share. 22 tests pin the reasoning, the convergence property, the AI rules and the API.

**The product.** A full product site built from user-journey research: Home, Product, For Artists, For Platforms, Developers (API reference with a live console), Technology (architecture, MeTTa rules, worked NAL example), Trust And Safety, About, and a guided, bilingual demo.

**What's next.** More than two writers; audio evidence as truth-valued input; live capture during the session; royalty statements checked against the signed split; collecting-society export; on-device agents; Incantio and BASIX.MARKET integration (concepts pending partner confirmation).
